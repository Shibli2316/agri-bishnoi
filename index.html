About

Who I am

I'm Rajesh Kumar Bishnoi — pursuing M.Sc. in Sustainable International Agriculture (Organic Agriculture) at the University of Göttingen & Kassel, based in Göttingen. With 4+ years across rural development, livelihood activities, and project management, I've lived in the field — from paddy belts in Bihar to maize fields in Southern Rajasthan.

Beyond the corporate world, I'm a part-time businessman, a researcher, and rational thinker who builds bridges in agriculture with modern communication to support smallholders.

I think in strategy, speak as a common man, and believe every smallholder's story deserves to be heard.
